# Word Choices and Framing Mechanisms

Use when choosing synonyms, naming features/proposals, or tightening prose.

## High-Leverage Substitutions
* **Results > Process:** `"cut p99 to 110ms"` > `"added debounce wrapper"`
* **Value > Cost:** Frame by reader gain vs outlay.
* **Investment > Spending:** `"invest a day in test harness"` > `"spend a day"`
* **Personalize the Number:** `"saves each on-call engineer 20m/page"` > `"reduces MTTR 14%"`
* **Opportunity > Fairness:** Expansive, non-alienating framing.
* **Certified > Attested:** Implies verified process and guarantee.
* **Efficiency > Conservation:** Innovation/gain > sacrifice/loss.
* **Human > Ledger Word:** Plain human-scale words over accounting jargon (`deliverer` > `provider`, `easy to understand` > `comprehensive`).
* **Repeatable Names:** Pick feature and service names a teammate can easily say and repeat in standup.

## Word Cautions
* **Aspirational Words:** `imagine`, `hassle-free`, `accountability`, `innovation`, `renew`, `rebuild`, `efficient`, `certified`, `balanced approach`.
* **Avoid `"we hold ourselves accountable"`** in reassurance contexts (plants failure). Use `"we deliver"`.
* **Avoid `"culture of..."`** (tarring pattern).

## Framing Rule
* **Do not adopt opposing vocabulary.** Using an opponent's/reviewer's negative framing accepts their definition. Define your own terms.

## Prose Craft
* **Front-load & End-load:** Put highest-priority point first; memorable line last.
* **One idea per message:** Restrict PRs/tickets to a single primary takeaway.
* **Sentence limit:** Max two commas per sentence.
* **Enumerate:** Use numbered lists for structure and substance.
* **Respond in writing:** Unanswered objections are assumed valid; first-mover frames the debate.
* **Quantify impact:** Show impact with numbers instead of debating labels ("crisis" vs "issue").
* **Everyday Asks:** Tie requests to reader-centric reasons; keep Slack/email asks to ~4 sentences.
* **Handling Mistakes:** Acknowledge errors fully up front; arguing causes others to dig in.
